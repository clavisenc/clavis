03One-time

Why one-time, not a subscription?

Most encryption tools bill you every year. Clavis Pro and Premium are single payments — you buy it once and it's yours, including future updates. It's an app that runs on your PC, not a service you rent.

  • Pay once, keep it. No renewals, no expiry date.
  • Every future update included. New features arrive inside the app.
  • Works offline, no account. Your licence is confirmed when you activate it and again when Clavis starts while online; offline, your plan keeps working.
  • Grow later. Move from Pro to Premium by paying only the difference.

04Before you buy

Questions

More answers in the Help centre, or write to hello@clavisenc.com.

What's actually free?

Encrypting and decrypting individual files with full-strength AES-256-GCM, one vault, the recovery key, integrity checks, secure delete and shredding files you no longer need. The security is identical to Pro — Pro adds power features, never stronger crypto.

What's the difference between Pro and Premium?

Pro is for working with lots of files on your PC: folders, several vaults, watch folders, key-file 2FA, secure notes, opening files without leaving a decrypted copy and browsing encrypted folders. Premium adds everything that reaches beyond one PC: encrypting straight into your cloud folder, the command line for scripts and scheduled backups, sharing encrypted files with other people, team folders your family or office share in the cloud, one-file vault backups and previous versions of files you edit.

Can I upgrade from Pro to Premium later?

Yes, and you only pay the difference. Email hello@clavisenc.com from the address you bought Pro with and we'll send you an upgrade code.

How do I buy Pro or Premium?

Press Buy Pro or Buy Premium above. Checkout is run by our payment partner, Dodo Payments (the merchant of record), and accepts cards, Apple Pay and UPI where available. Prices include tax. Your license key arrives by email right after payment.

How do I activate Pro or Premium?

Your license key will arrive by email right after you buy. Open Clavis → Settings → License → paste the key → Activate. The key is checked when you activate it and again each time Clavis starts while online; offline, your plan keeps working, with no account.

One licence, how many PCs?

Your license works on up to two of your own computers at a time. To move it to a new PC, open Settings → License → Remove license on the old one first, then activate on the new one.

Do you offer discounts for organisations or bulk purchases?

Yes: 15% off from 3 licences and 25% off from 10, with invoices for your organisation. 25 or more: email us for a quote. See Clavis for teams.

Can I try before I buy?

Yes. Clavis Free gives you the full app and full-strength encryption. To try Pro or Premium, email hello@clavisenc.com with your device code (Settings → License) and you'll get a trial key for that PC. Because you can try everything for free, Pro purchases are final.

Do I have to pay for updates?

No. Pro and Premium are one-time and include all future updates.

Can I get a refund?

Clavis Free is the trial, so purchases are final and we don't refund a change of mind. We do refund double charges, a license key we can't make work, and wherever the law requires. See the Refund Policy.
