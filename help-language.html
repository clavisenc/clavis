Change the language

Clavis is available in English, Français, Deutsch, தமிழ் (Tamil) and हिन्दी (Hindi). By default it follows your system language.

Choose a language in Settings → Language, press Save, then restart Clavis.

The translations are first drafts and some windows are still in English. Spotted a mistake, or want to help with your language? Email hello@clavisenc.com.
