Refund Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Short version: Clavis Free is the trial. Use it for as long as you like before you buy. Clavis Pro and Clavis Premium are digital licences delivered instantly by email, so purchases are final and we don't refund a change of mind. We do refund if something went wrong on our side, as described below.

Try before you buy

Clavis Free has the full app and the same encryption as the paid plans, with no time limit and no account. The paid plans only add features on top. Want to try Pro or Premium first? Email hello@clavisenc.com with your device code (Clavis → Settings → License) and we'll send you a free trial key.

When we refund

We give a full refund when:

  • You were charged more than once for the same licence.
  • Your licence key doesn't work and we can't fix it within 14 days of your purchase. Email us first: most activation problems are solved in a reply.
  • The law where you live requires it. Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under your local consumer law.

Because a licence is delivered instantly and can't be returned, we don't refund purchases made by mistake or a change of mind once the key has been sent.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email hello@clavisenc.com within 14 days of your purchase.
  2. Include your order number (it's in your receipt email) and what went wrong.
  3. We reply within 3 business days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method; how long that takes depends on your bank or card provider.

When a refund is made, the licence key is switched off and the PCs using it return to Clavis Free. Your files are not affected: everything you encrypted still opens with your password on the free plan.

Who processes payments

Orders are processed by our payment partner, which acts as the merchant of record and is named on your checkout and receipt. Prices include applicable taxes.

Contact

Questions? Email hello@clavisenc.com. See also our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
