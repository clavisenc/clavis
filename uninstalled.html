Clavis Encrypt has been uninstalled

Your encrypted files and your vault are still on this PC.

Uninstalling removed the app, nothing else. Your .enc files are where you left them, and your vault (your recovery key, Clavis ID, contacts, secure notes and settings) stays in the folder %USERPROFILE%\.fileencryptionapp, for example C:\Users\you\.fileencryptionapp.

Opening your files

  • Reinstall Clavis Encrypt and everything is back as it was: same vault, same password, same files. Download it again.
  • Without reinstalling, each .enc file opens with your vault password (and your key file, if you used one): in your browser on any phone, Mac or PC, or with the free open-source decryptor.
  • Files someone else encrypted for you (Encrypt for someone) open only in Clavis Encrypt, with the Clavis ID in your vault.

Thinking of deleting the vault folder?

Please decrypt everything you still need first. Without the vault folder:

  • your recovery key no longer works, so if you forget your password, your encrypted files can't be opened by anyone, including us;
  • files encrypted for you, your secure notes and your contacts are gone, unless you made a vault backup (.clavisbackup).

If you're not sure, leave the folder where it is. It's small, and it lets a reinstall pick up exactly where you left off.
