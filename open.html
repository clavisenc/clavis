    Good to know

    • Opening takes a few seconds: the same deliberate slowness that protects your password from guessing.
    • Very large files (over about 1 GB) may not fit in a phone's memory; open those on a computer.
    • Files made with Encrypt for someone open only in Clavis Encrypt, with the recipient's Clavis ID.
    • This page works the same way as the free open-source decryptor; the file format is public.
    • To send a file back locked with a password, encrypt a file in your browser, on this phone or computer.
    • For files you keep, whole folders, watch folders and more, get Clavis Encrypt.
