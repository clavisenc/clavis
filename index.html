Step one

Point it at what matters.

Drag a file or folder onto Clavis, or right-click it in File Explorer. Nothing is uploaded.

Step two

Turn the key.

Your password becomes a 256-bit key through Argon2id. Every 64 KiB piece is sealed with AES-256-GCM and checked when you open it.

Step three

Locked, and still yours.

Open a locked file in its usual app, put it in OneDrive, or send it to a contact. No decrypted copy is left behind.
