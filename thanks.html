Your order is complete

Your license key is on its way. It arrives by email within a few minutes, from Dodo Payments, our payment partner. Check your spam or promotions folder if you don't see it.

Activate it in three steps

  1. Open Clavis Encrypt. Don't have it yet? Download it free, or update to version 3.16.1 or later (Settings → Updates → Check now). Bought Premium for the command line, or Pro for a USB stick? Use the installer: the Microsoft Store version doesn't include those two, or the right-click menu.
  2. Go to Settings → License → Enter license key.
  3. Paste the key from the email and press Activate. Your plan unlocks straight away.

The key works on up to two PCs. To move it to a new PC, choose Remove license on the old one first.

Need help?

No email after 15 minutes, or the key won't activate? Email hello@clavisenc.com with your order number (from the receipt) and we'll sort it out. See also the Refund Policy.
