Your license key is on its way. It arrives by email within a few minutes, from Dodo Payments, our payment partner. Check your spam or promotions folder if you don't see it.

Activate it in three steps

  1. Open Clavis Encrypt. Don't have it yet? Download it free, or update to version 3.16.1 or later (Settings → Updates → Check now).
  2. Go to Settings → License → Enter license key.
  3. Paste the key from the email and press Activate. Your plan unlocks straight away.

The key works on up to two PCs. To move it to a new PC, choose Remove license on the old one first.
