Clavis Free vs Pro vs Premium: which do you need?

The short version: the encryption is the same in every edition. Free isn't a weaker version — it uses the same AES-256-GCM, Argon2id and post-quantum recovery key. Paying buys convenience and reach, never safety.

Free — for encrypting your private files

  • Encrypt, decrypt and verify files, one or many at once, with full-strength encryption
  • One vault, with a post-quantum recovery key
  • The right-click menu, Activity history, auto-lock
  • A Clavis ID: receive and open files people share with you
  • Send with a password: a locked copy anyone can open in their browser
  • Unlock with Windows Hello (face, fingerprint or PIN)
  • Shred files and folders (best effort on SSDs and flash)
  • Restoring a vault backup
  • Adding a page to a USB stick that opens your files in a browser on other PCs

Never paywalled: restoring backups, opening shared files, your recovery key, or any security feature. And your files never depend on a plan, or even on the app: the file format is public, with a free open-source decryptor and an in-browser opener.

Pro ($19, one-time) — for lots of files on one PC

  • Encrypt whole folders, subfolders included, in one step
  • Find sensitive files: helps you find ID and bank documents on your PC, then encrypt the ones you tick in one go
  • Open encrypted files without leaving a decrypted copy
  • Browse encrypted folders by real file names
  • Watch folders that encrypt new files automatically
  • Secure notes, multiple vaults and key-file 2FA
  • Clavis on a USB stick: run Clavis from a stick on other Windows PCs, without installing anything there

Premium ($29, one-time) — for everything beyond one PC

  • Everything in Pro
  • Encrypt straight into OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox
  • Encrypt files for other people — no password shared
  • One-file vault backups and previous versions of edited files
  • The command line for scripts and scheduled backups

How to choose

  • You encrypt the odd document or photo: Free is all you need.
  • You keep a whole folder of private files and open them often: Pro.
  • Your files live in the cloud, you share them, or you want backups and scripts: Premium.

Both paid editions are one-time purchases with future updates included, and you can upgrade from Pro to Premium by paying the difference. See pricing — Pro and Premium go on sale soon.
