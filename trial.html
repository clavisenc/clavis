Questions

What happens when the 7 days end?

Clavis goes back to Free. Your encrypted files and vaults stay exactly as they are, and Free still encrypts and decrypts your files with the same encryption. To keep the Pro or Premium features, buy a licence: it's a one-time purchase with no subscription.

Can I try both Pro and Premium?

Each PC gets one free trial. Premium includes everything in Pro, so choose Premium to try it all.

Do I need an account or an email address?

No. You only paste This PC's code. It's a short code made from your PC's Windows or Linux ID: it says nothing about you or your files, and the key works only on that PC.

Where do I find This PC's code?

Open Clavis Encrypt and go to Settings → License. The code is 8 characters, like K7Q2-M9XD. If you don't see it, update Clavis (Settings → Updates → Check now).

I'm trying it for my team. Can several PCs have a trial?

Yes, each PC can get its own 7-day trial key here. For team prices and invoices, see Clavis for teams.
