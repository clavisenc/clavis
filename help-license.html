Help · Settings & troubleshooting · Free

Activate Clavis Pro or Premium

Clavis Pro and Premium are one-time purchases — see pricing. Your licence key arrives by email.

Activate

Open Settings → License, paste the key and press Activate.

The key is checked when you activate it and again each time Clavis starts while online; offline, your plan keeps working, with no account. Once active, Settings shows your plan and who it's licensed to.

Gift and trial keys

A key you got as a gift or a trial works on one PC only. Before the person makes it, send them the code shown under This PC's code in Settings → License (press Copy). Paste the key you get back and press Activate. A trial key also shows its end date; after it, Clavis goes back to Free and your files and vaults stay exactly as they are.

Moving to another PC

Press Remove license on the old PC, then activate the key on the new one. Your files and vaults are not affected by licence changes — and restoring a backup, opening shared files and the encryption itself stay free whatever your plan.

Upgrading from Pro to Premium

Email hello@clavisenc.com from the address you bought Pro with; you only pay the difference. Paste the Premium key into the same field.
