The short version: Clavis runs entirely on your computer. It does not collect, transmit or store your files, your passwords, your recovery keys, your activity or your personal data. There is no account. Apart from buying a licence (see Payments), the only personal detail we may ask for is your email address, and giving it is optional (for example, after a download from our website has started). What follows is the detail.

Your files and passwords

Encryption and decryption happen locally on your PC. Your files never leave your computer, and your password and recovery key are never transmitted or stored anywhere by us — we never see them and cannot recover them.

When the app goes online

Clavis contacts the internet on its own only to check for updates and, on Pro or Premium, to confirm your licence (see License activation below). For updates, it requests a small update manifest and, if you choose to update, the update files, from our public GitHub release repository. As with any web request, the server and its content-delivery network can see your IP address and the request; no personal data or file content is sent. You can turn automatic update checks off in Settings.

Clavis Encrypt collects nothing about how you use it. There is no usage tracking, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising ID in the app. The Activity list (what you encrypted and when) is stored only on your own PC and is never sent anywhere. Apart from the update check above and the licence check below, the app does not connect to the internet at all.

License activation

When you activate Clavis Pro or Premium with a key bought from our store, Clavis sends that key to our payment provider, Dodo Payments, to confirm it's genuine and belongs to Clavis Pro or Premium. The activation is recorded under a neutral label ("Clavis for Windows") — no computer name, user name or anything about your files. After that, Pro works offline and needs no account.

Each time Clavis starts while you're online, it re-confirms the key with Dodo Payments; if you're offline nothing happens and your plan keeps working. Choosing Remove license tells Dodo Payments to free that device slot; if you're offline at that moment, Clavis frees it the next time it starts online. License keys issued directly by us are verified entirely offline and never sent anywhere.

Free trial keys

On our trial page you paste This PC's code from Clavis and choose a plan. The page sends only that code and the plan to our trial service (run on Cloudflare), which signs a 7-day key that works on that one PC. The code is a short fingerprint of your PC's operating-system ID; it says nothing about you, your files or your computer.

So that each PC gets one trial, we keep a one-way fingerprint of the code (not the code itself), the plan, the start and end dates, and the key, so we can show the same key again if you ask during the trial. We don't ask for your name or email address, we don't set cookies, and we don't store your IP address.

This website

Our website uses privacy-friendly analytics (GoatCounter) that set no cookies. Ordinary visits are counted anonymously and only in totals: page views, referring sites, countries, and how far pages are read, to understand what's useful. The one exception is links in our emails to businesses (see Links in our emails to businesses below). The encrypt and open pages count only visits and how many files were encrypted or opened, never file names, sizes, contents or passwords. A few pages keep a small yes/no note in your browser so they don't ask you the same thing twice (for example, once you've given an email for the download guide); these notes contain no personal data and are never sent to us. There is no cross-site tracking and no advertising.

Links in our emails to businesses. When we email a business about Clavis, the clavisenc.com links in that email carry a short code made for that business, for example ?f=k7q2m, and the email says so. If someone opens one of those links and then uses the page, our site sends GoatCounter an event with that code: which of our pages was opened, which buttons were clicked (for example Download, Buy or Ask for a quote), and whether a trial key was given out during that visit. We use this only to see which of the businesses we wrote to took a look, so we know whom to follow up with. The code is removed from the address bar straight away and kept only in the open page while you move between our pages: no cookies and no browser storage (the address you opened may still be in your browser history). GoatCounter doesn't know which business a code belongs to; as with any visit, it also receives technical details such as your IP address (used to look up the country), your browser and your screen size. The list that matches codes to businesses stays with us, and our own alerts show only the code, the country and the kind of business. Don't want this? Remove the f=… part of the link before you open it, or email hello@clavisenc.com and we'll delete the link between your business and its code and won't put a code in our emails to you again. Visits that don't come from one of those emails are counted only anonymously.

When you uninstall

When you uninstall the Windows version installed with our installer (not the Microsoft Store version), the uninstaller opens a page on our website in your browser, with the version you had installed in its address. That visit is counted like any other page view on this site: no cookies, no IP address stored. The page asks why you uninstalled; answering is optional and anonymous, and nothing is sent unless you click an answer. Silent uninstalls (for example by an IT department) and updates to a newer version don't open the page. The app itself still sends nothing.

Your email address

When you download. After a download starts on our website, we offer to email you a short setup guide. On a phone, you can instead ask us to email you the download link for your PC. Giving your email address is optional, and the download never depends on it. If you give it, that is the only thing we collect: not your name, not anything about your computer, and nothing from the app. We use it for two things only: to keep a record of who asked for the guide, and to email you that short setup guide or the download link you asked for, and important notices about the app (for example a security update).

News and offers are optional. We only send you news about new versions and Clavis Encrypt offers if you tick the box for them, or sign up for them on our Pricing page (where you confirm by clicking a link we email you).

Who sees it. We never sell or rent your email address. We share it only with the services that help us send and promote Clavis Encrypt, and only for that purpose. Today that is Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), which stores our mailing list and sends our emails.

Your choice, any time. Every email has an unsubscribe link. To have your address deleted completely, write to hello@clavisenc.com.

Payments

When you buy Clavis Pro or Premium, payment is processed by Dodo Payments, which acts as the merchant of record (it sells the license to you and handles tax, invoices and refunds on our behalf). We do not receive or store your card details. The provider processes the information needed to complete your purchase (such as your email and payment details) under its own privacy policy, and sends us the information needed to issue your license and provide support.

If you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and use it only to help you.

Children

Clavis is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@clavisenc.com.
