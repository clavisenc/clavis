Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

The short version: Clavis runs entirely on your computer. It does not collect, transmit or store your files, your passwords, your recovery keys, your activity or your personal data. There is no account. The only personal detail we ask for is your email address when you download from our website. What follows is the detail.

Your files and passwords

Encryption and decryption happen locally on your PC. Your files never leave your computer, and your password and recovery key are never transmitted or stored anywhere by us — we never see them and cannot recover them.

When the app goes online

The only time Clavis contacts the internet on its own is to check for updates. It requests a small update manifest and, if you choose to update, the update files, from our public GitHub release repository. As with any web request, the server and its content-delivery network can see your IP address and the request; no personal data or file content is sent. You can turn automatic update checks off in Settings.

Clavis Encrypt collects nothing about how you use it. There is no usage tracking, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising ID in the app. The Activity list (what you encrypted and when) is stored only on your own PC and is never sent anywhere. Apart from the update check above and the licence check below, the app does not connect to the internet at all.

License activation

When you activate Clavis Pro with a key bought from our store, Clavis sends that key once to our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, to confirm it's genuine and belongs to Clavis Pro. The activation is recorded under a neutral label ("Clavis for Windows") — no computer name, user name or anything about your files. After that, Pro works offline and needs no account.

Each time Clavis starts while you're online, it re-confirms the key with Lemon Squeezy; if you're offline nothing happens and Pro keeps working. Choosing Remove license tells Lemon Squeezy to free that device slot; if you're offline at that moment, Clavis frees it the next time it starts online. License keys issued directly by us are verified entirely offline and never sent anywhere.

This website

Our website uses privacy-friendly analytics (GoatCounter) that set no cookies and collect no personal data. We see only aggregate, anonymous figures — page views, referring sites, countries, and how far pages are read — to understand what's useful. The encrypt and open pages count only visits and how many files were encrypted or opened, never file names, sizes, contents or passwords. There is no cross-site tracking and no advertising.

When you uninstall

When you uninstall the Windows version installed with our installer (not the Microsoft Store version), the uninstaller opens a page on our website in your browser, with the version you had installed in its address. That visit is counted like any other page view on this site: no cookies, no IP address stored. The page asks why you uninstalled; answering is optional and anonymous, and nothing is sent unless you click an answer. Silent uninstalls (for example by an IT department) and updates to a newer version don't open the page. The app itself still sends nothing.

Your email address

When you download. After a download starts on our website, we offer to email you a short setup guide. Giving your email address is optional, and the download never depends on it. If you give it, that is the only thing we collect: not your name, not anything about your computer, and nothing from the app. We use it for two things only: to keep a record of who asked for the guide, and to email you that short setup guide and important notices about the app (for example a security update).

News and offers are optional. We only send you news about new versions and Clavis Encrypt offers if you tick the box for them, or sign up for them on our Pricing page (where you confirm by clicking a link we email you).

Who sees it. We never sell or rent your email address. We share it only with the services that help us send and promote Clavis Encrypt, and only for that purpose. Today that is Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), which stores our mailing list and sends our emails.

Your choice, any time. Every email has an unsubscribe link. To have your address deleted completely, write to hello@clavisenc.com.

Payments

When you buy Clavis Pro, payment is processed by a third-party payment provider. We do not receive or store your card details. The provider processes the information needed to complete your purchase (such as your email and payment details) under its own privacy policy, and sends us the information needed to issue your license and provide support.

If you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and use it only to help you.

Children

Clavis is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@clavisenc.com.
